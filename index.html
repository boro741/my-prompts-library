Prompt 01
⚡ Quick TL;DR
Quick
Best when you need the core idea in under a minute. Use this before deciding whether to watch a video.
prompt
Give me a quick understanding of this video. Don't describe the video or tell me what the speaker did. Instead, explain the actual ideas from the video directly. Use simple, everyday language. Give me: The Big Idea Explain the main idea in 3–5 sentences. The Important Points List the 5 most important ideas I should know. The Takeaway Tell me the one thing I should remember. Ignore greetings, introductions, sponsorships, filler, repetition, and promotional content. Keep it short and easy to understand.
Prompt 02
📝 Simple Summary
Quick Learn
A slightly deeper version of TL;DR. Explains each idea with why it matters and connects them together.
prompt
Explain this video to me in simple language. Don't give me a report about the video. Instead, teach me the actual ideas being discussed. Start with the big picture, then explain the important ideas one by one. For each important idea, tell me: What it means Why it matters How it works A useful example from the video Show me how the ideas connect with each other. Finish with the most important things I should remember. Do not use phrases like: "In this video..." "The speaker explains..." "The creator talks about..." Explain the subject directly. Remove filler, repetition, greetings, sponsorships, and promotional content.
Prompt 03
🧠 Teach Me
Learn ⭐ Recommended
One of the strongest general-purpose prompts. Starts from basics, builds up, and explains reasoning — not just what was said.
prompt
Teach me the subject covered in this video. Assume I have not watched the video and may be new to the topic. Start with the basic idea and slowly build toward the more advanced ideas. Explain things in the order that makes them easiest to understand. For every important concept: Explain it in simple language Explain why it is needed Explain how it works Give the example used in the video Connect it to what we just learned Don't just tell me what was said. Help me actually understand it. Use simple words and short explanations. Explain technical terms when they first appear. At the end, give me: The big picture The 5–10 most important ideas What I should remember Avoid phrases like "the speaker says" or "the video explains." Teach me the subject directly.
Prompt 04
📺 Learn Without Watching
Deep ⭐ Recommended
The "replace watching" prompt. Ideal for long videos — preserves the full logical flow so you get the same understanding as watching it.
prompt
Help me understand this video without having to watch the entire thing. Turn the important content into a clear, easy-to-read explanation. I want to understand: What problem is being discussed Why the problem matters The important ideas How those ideas work Why the suggested approach makes sense Examples used to explain the ideas Important trade-offs or limitations The final conclusion Keep the logical flow of the original discussion. Don't write a report about the video. Instead, explain the subject directly, as if you are teaching me. Keep important details and context, but remove: Filler Repetition Greetings Sponsorships Advertisements Unrelated discussion The final result should give me the same important understanding I would get from watching the video.
Prompt 05
🗺️ Mental Model
Deep ⭐ Recommended
For understanding how ideas connect, not just what they are. Excellent for technical, conceptual, or architecture videos.
prompt
Help me build a clear mental model of the subject in this video. Don't just list the points. Show me how the ideas connect. Explain: The Problem What are we trying to solve? The Core Idea What is the main idea? The Important Pieces What are the important concepts or components? How They Connect Explain how the pieces work together. Cause and Effect Explain what causes what and why. Trade-offs What do we gain and what do we give up? Examples Use the examples from the video to make the ideas easier to understand. The Complete Picture Bring everything together into one simple mental model. Use simple language. The goal is that I can close the answer and explain the topic in my own words.
Prompt 06
📚 Study Notes
Deep
Creates notes you can save in your knowledge base and understand months later — no video needed.
prompt
Turn this video into useful study notes. Write the notes so that I can come back to them months later and still understand the topic without watching the video again. Use this structure: Overview Explain the big picture. Key Concepts Explain the important concepts in simple language. Detailed Explanation Walk through the important ideas and reasoning. Examples Include useful examples and analogies. Important Distinctions Point out things that are easy to confuse. Practical Applications Explain where the ideas can be useful. Mistakes and Misconceptions Include important mistakes or misconceptions discussed. Key Takeaways Give me the most important lessons. Don't describe the video. Teach me the subject directly. Remove filler and repetition, but keep important context.
Prompt 07
🗂️ Chapter by Chapter
Deep
Excellent for long videos. Breaks it into logical chapters and teaches the content of each one with context intact.
prompt
Break this video into clear logical chapters and teach me the content of each chapter. Don't simply tell me what happens in each part. Explain the actual ideas being taught. For every chapter: [Chapter Name] Explain: What we are trying to understand The important concepts The reasoning Examples Important details How this connects to the previous chapter If timestamps are available, include them. Start with a short overview of the whole topic. At the end, explain how all the chapters connect to form the complete picture. Use simple language and remove filler, repetition, sponsorships, and unrelated discussion.
Prompt 08
🔍 Critical Analysis
Analysis
Goes beyond summarizing — explains the content first, then critically examines the claims, assumptions, and weaknesses.
prompt
First, help me understand the ideas presented in this video. Explain the ideas directly and clearly, without describing what the speaker is doing. Once the explanation is complete, analyze the ideas critically. Main Ideas What are the important ideas? Reasoning Why do these ideas make sense? Evidence What evidence or examples support them? Assumptions What assumptions are being made? Strengths What is convincing? Weaknesses What may be incomplete, unclear, or questionable? Alternative Views Are there other reasonable ways to look at the topic? Things to Verify Which claims would be worth checking independently? Clearly separate the explanation of the video's content from the critical analysis. Do not change the meaning of the original ideas.
Prompt 09
✅ Actionable Summary
Quick Learn
For productivity, business, self-improvement, and tutorial videos. Focuses on what you can actually do with the knowledge.
prompt
Turn the useful ideas from this video into practical actions. First, explain the main idea in simple language. Then organize the useful information into: What Matters What are the most important ideas? What Should I Do? Turn the recommendations into clear actions. For each action explain: What to do Why it matters How to do it When it is useful Examples Show how these ideas can be applied using examples from the video. Mistakes to Avoid Explain the mistakes or wrong approaches discussed. Key Takeaways Give me the most important things to remember. Do not describe the video. Teach me the ideas directly. Only include recommendations that come from the video or are clearly supported by it.
Prompt 10
💬 Question & Answer
Learn ⭐ Recommended
Organizes the video around natural questions. Great format for complicated videos — feels like a conversation, not a report.
prompt
Turn the important ideas from this video into questions and answers. The questions should be natural questions that someone learning this topic would actually ask. Start with: The Big Picture Give me a short explanation of the main idea. Then create: Q: [Question] Answer Explain the answer simply. Why? Explain the reasoning. Example Give the relevant example from the video. Remember Give me the key point in one sentence. Arrange the questions in a logical order so that each answer prepares me for the next question. Finish with: The 5 Things I Should Remember Don't create unnecessary questions just to make the answer longer. The goal is to make a complicated topic feel like a simple conversation.
Prompt 11
📊 Three Levels of Understanding
Learn Deep
One prompt, three depths — 30 seconds, 5 minutes, full understanding. You choose how deep to go after seeing each level.
prompt
Explain this video at three levels. Level 1 — 30 Seconds Give me the core idea in 3–5 sentences. Level 2 — 5 Minutes Explain the main concepts, reasoning, examples, and conclusions. Level 3 — Full Understanding Give me a complete explanation. Include: Background Important concepts Examples Reasoning Connections Trade-offs Limitations Important details Final conclusions Keep all three levels consistent. Level 2 should add detail to Level 1. Level 3 should build on Level 2. Don't describe the video. Teach me the subject directly. Use simple language throughout.
Prompt 12
🌱 Beginner Mode
Learn
Great when the video assumes too much knowledge. Starts from zero, builds up, explains every term without dumbing it down.
prompt
Teach me the subject of this video as if I am completely new to it. Assume I am intelligent but have no background knowledge. Start with the simplest idea. Then gradually build toward the more advanced concepts. Whenever you introduce: A technical term An acronym A new concept An assumption explain it in simple language first. Use examples and analogies when they make the idea easier to understand. Don't oversimplify important technical details. The goal is: "After reading this, I should understand the topic well enough to explain it to another person." Don't summarize what the speaker said. Actually teach me what I need to understand.
Prompt 13
🗃️ Extract the Knowledge
Analysis Deep
Treats the video like a structured knowledge source. Separates facts, opinions, principles, arguments, and recommendations cleanly.
prompt
Extract the useful knowledge from this video and organize it so I can learn it easily. Separate it into: Concepts What concepts do I need to understand? Facts What important facts or information should I know? Principles What general principles can I take away? Examples What examples make the concepts easier to understand? Arguments What reasoning is being used? Opinions Which ideas are opinions or personal interpretations? Recommendations What actions or approaches are recommended? Conclusions What should I understand after putting everything together? Keep facts, opinions, and interpretations separate. Don't describe what happened in the video. Explain the knowledge directly.
Prompt 14
🕸️ Connect the Ideas
Analysis Deep
Maps relationships between concepts — cause/effect, problem/solution, trade-offs, dependencies. Builds one connected picture.
prompt
Help me understand how the important ideas in this video connect to each other. Don't just give me a list. Build one connected explanation. Show me relationships such as: Problem → Solution Cause → Effect Concept → Example Component → Responsibility Decision → Reason Benefit → Trade-off Dependency → Result For example, explain relationships naturally: "Because X creates this problem, we use Y. Y solves that problem by doing Z. However, this introduces another trade-off..." Start with the core problem and gradually build the complete picture. Use simple language. The goal is for me to finish with a clear mental map of the subject.
Prompt 15
🔧 Technical / Engineering
Technical ⭐ Recommended
Specialized for architecture, system design, APIs, data flows, queues, databases, and engineering videos. Covers everything a dev needs.
prompt
Teach me the technical subject covered in this video. I want to understand the system, not just get a summary. Use simple language but keep the important technical details. 1. The Big Picture What are we trying to build or solve? 2. The Problem Why does this problem exist? 3. Architecture What are the main components? What is each component responsible for? How do they connect? 4. End-to-End Flow Walk me through what happens step by step. 5. APIs If APIs are discussed, explain: Who calls the API Which endpoint is called What request is sent What response comes back Why the API is needed 6. Data Flow Explain how data moves through the system. 7. Messaging If queues, topics, events, or messaging are discussed, explain: Who publishes What is published Who consumes it Why messaging is used What happens afterward 8. Database / Cache / Storage Explain how each one is used and why. 9. Design Decisions Explain why the system is designed this way. 10. Trade-offs Explain what we gain and what we sacrifice. 11. Examples Include useful examples from the video. 12. Complete Picture Bring everything together into one end-to-end explanation. 13. Key Takeaways Give me the most important technical lessons. Don't say "the speaker explains" or "the video discusses." Teach me the technical subject directly. Don't invent technical details that aren't present in the video. If something isn't clear from the video, say that it isn't clear.
Prompt 16
🎙️ Podcast / Interview Distiller
Deep Analysis
For multi-speaker conversations. Regroups a rambling 2-hour episode by theme instead of by time, and separates who believes what.
prompt
This video is a conversation, interview, or podcast with more than one person. Don't give me a timeline of who said what. Instead, extract the actual ideas and organize them by topic, not by time. Give me: The Core Themes What were the 3–6 main topics actually discussed? For each theme: The main idea The strongest points made Where the people agreed Where they disagreed, and why The best story, example, or anecdote used Each Person's Viewpoint Summarize what each speaker believes and what makes their view different from the others. Surprising or Counterintuitive Ideas What was said that goes against common thinking? Quotable Ideas 3–5 ideas worth remembering, one sentence each. What Was Left Unresolved Questions that were raised but never answered. Skip small talk, intros, ads, tangents, and repeated points. Explain the ideas directly, in simple language.
Prompt 17
🛠️ Tutorial → Step-by-Step Guide
Technical ⭐ Recommended
Turns any how-to into a written guide you can follow without pausing and rewinding. Forces exact commands, code, and expected results.
prompt
This video is a tutorial or how-to. Turn it into a written guide I can follow without watching the video. Give me: Goal What will I have built or achieved at the end? Prerequisites Tools, accounts, versions, files, or knowledge I need before starting. Steps Number every step in order. For each step: What to do (exact commands, code, settings, or clicks) Why this step is needed What I should see when it works Code and Commands Write out all code, commands, and configuration exactly as shown. Don't paraphrase them. Common Errors Mistakes the video warns about, and how to fix them. Verification How do I confirm everything is working at the end? Variations Any alternatives or optional steps that were mentioned. If something shown on screen is not clearly explained, say so instead of guessing. Don't describe the video. Write the guide directly.
Prompt 18
📰 Fact-Check the Claims
Analysis
For finance, health, news, and "shocking facts" videos. Lists every claim, rates how well it's supported, and flags sponsorship bias.
prompt
List every factual claim made in this video, then help me judge how much to trust it. Don't describe the video. Focus on the claims. For each claim: Claim State it in one clear sentence. Type Fact, statistic, prediction, opinion, or personal experience? Support Given What evidence, source, or reasoning was offered in the video, if any? Confidence Based only on what the video gave me: High / Medium / Low, and why. How to Verify What would I search for or check to confirm this independently? At the end give me: The Claims That Matter Most Which 3–5 claims does the whole argument depend on? Red Flags Signs of exaggeration, cherry-picking, missing context, or conflicts of interest (sponsorships, products being sold, affiliate links). Bottom Line What can I reasonably believe after this video, and what should I hold loosely? Don't add facts from outside the video unless you clearly label them as outside information.
Prompt 19
🧪 Quiz Me
Learn ⭐ Recommended
The only prompt that tests you instead of telling you. Use it after any other prompt — retrieval is what makes knowledge stick.
prompt
I want to test whether I actually understood this video. First, give me a short recap of the main ideas (5–8 sentences) to refresh my memory. Then create a quiz based only on the content of the video. Make: 5 Basic Questions Test whether I remember the key facts and definitions. 5 Understanding Questions Test whether I understand why things work, not just what they are. 3 Application Questions Give me a new situation and ask how the ideas from the video would apply. 2 "Explain It" Questions Ask me to explain a concept in my own words. Put all the answers in a separate section at the end, not next to the questions. For each answer, explain why it is correct in 1–3 sentences. Finally, give me: Flashcards 10 short question → answer pairs I can copy into a flashcard app. Keep the questions clear. Don't make them tricky just to be hard.
Prompt 20
⚖️ Compare & Decide
Analysis Quick
For reviews and "X vs Y" videos. Produces a criteria table, "choose X if…" guidance, and a bias check — so you can actually decide.
prompt
This video compares or reviews options (products, tools, methods, approaches, or ideas). Help me make a decision, not just understand the video. Give me: What Is Being Compared List the options. The Criteria What factors does the video use to judge them? If an important factor was ignored, add it and label it as my addition. Comparison Table Options as columns, criteria as rows. Fill it with what the video actually says. Mark unknown cells as "not covered". Strengths and Weaknesses For each option, the honest pros and cons from the video. The Video's Verdict What does the video recommend, and for whom? Who Should Choose What "Choose X if you...", "Choose Y if you..." Bias Check Is there a sponsorship, affiliate link, or obvious personal preference that might influence the verdict? Deal-breakers Anything that would rule an option out completely for certain people. Stay faithful to the video. Don't invent specs, prices, or features that were not mentioned.
Prompt 21
🧭 Should I Watch This?
Quick
A triage prompt. Tells you who the video is for, how much is filler, which timestamps matter, and gives a watch / skim / skip verdict.
prompt
Help me decide whether this video is worth my time before I watch it. Be honest and direct. Don't sell me the video. Tell me: In One Sentence What is this video really about? Who It Is For Who benefits most? Who will find it too basic or too advanced? What You Need to Know First Prerequisites or background knowledge assumed. What's Actually New Which ideas are genuinely valuable or hard to find elsewhere, versus common knowledge repeated? Signal vs Noise Roughly how much is useful content, and how much is intro, filler, promotion, or repetition? Best Parts If timestamps are available, the 2–4 sections worth jumping to directly. Skip These Sections that can be safely skipped. Verdict Watch fully / Watch selected parts / Just read a summary / Skip — and why, in 2–3 sentences. Keep the whole answer short. This is meant to save me time, not take more of it.
Prompt 22
🎓 Lecture → Exam Notes
Deep Learn
For academic lectures. Unlike Study Notes, this keeps exact definitions, formulas with every symbol explained, full derivations, and worked problems.
prompt
This video is an academic lecture or course video. Turn it into exam-ready notes. Use this structure: Topic and Scope What part of the subject does this lecture cover, and how does it fit into the bigger course? Definitions Every term that was defined, stated precisely, using the lecture's own wording where possible. Formulas, Rules, and Theorems Write each one out fully. Explain what every symbol or part means, and when it applies. Derivations and Reasoning Walk through the important derivations, proofs, or logical arguments step by step. Don't skip steps. Worked Examples Reproduce the examples or problems solved in the lecture, with each step explained. Common Mistakes Errors the lecturer warned about, or steps students often get wrong. Likely Exam Questions 5–8 questions this material could be tested on, with short model answers. Quick Revision Sheet A compact half-page summary I can review the night before an exam. Keep notation consistent with the lecture. If a step is not shown clearly in the video, say so rather than filling it in yourself.

Prompt 24 · Master
⭐ Master Prompt — Use This as Your Default
Learn Deep Technical ⭐ Best Overall
Combines every important element into one reusable prompt. Works for any video type. Use this when you don't want to pick a specific mode — it handles everything from basics to reasoning to trade-offs and practical takeaways.
prompt
Help me learn the subject of this video. I don't want a report about the video. Instead, turn the important content into a clear explanation that feels like I am learning the topic directly from the person who made the video. Don't say: "In this video..." "The speaker explains..." "The creator discusses..." "The video talks about..." Explain the actual ideas directly. Use simple, natural language. Start With the Big Picture First help me understand: What is the main topic? What problem are we trying to solve? Why does it matter? Build the Understanding Start from the basics and gradually move toward the more advanced ideas. For each important idea: What is it? Why do we need it? How does it work? Why does it matter? What example helps explain it? Connect each idea to the previous one so the explanation feels like one continuous lesson. Explain the Reasoning Don't just tell me what the solution or idea is. Explain why it makes sense. Help me understand the thinking behind important decisions, approaches, and conclusions. Use Examples Keep the useful examples, stories, analogies, demonstrations, and real-world situations from the video. Use them where they make the explanation easier to understand. Keep Important Details Don't remove details that are important for understanding. Keep things such as: Important facts Numbers Technical details Comparisons Assumptions Trade-offs Limitations Exceptions Important distinctions Connect Everything After explaining the individual ideas, show me how everything fits together. I want a connected mental model, not a list of disconnected points. Final Takeaways End with: The 5–10 Things I Should Remember Then give me: One-Minute Recall A short explanation that lets me quickly remember the whole topic later. Writing Style Use: Simple English Conversational language Short paragraphs Clear headings Examples Analogies when useful Bullets when useful Avoid: Formal academic language Unnecessary jargon Repetition Filler Long complicated sentences Important Stay faithful to the information in the video. Don't invent information. Don't change the meaning of the ideas. If something is unclear or missing, say so instead of guessing. Ignore greetings, filler, sponsorships, advertisements, repetition, and unrelated discussion. The goal is simple: When I finish reading your answer, I should feel that I actually learned the video rather than simply reading a summary of it.
